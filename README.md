## 👋 About Me

> I'm **Adeola**, a full-stack and mobile engineer. I build products **from database to App Store**, and I like the bit where they get real users.
>
> I started as a **Java instructor at NIIT** and a private tutor for web and mobile development. That's where I learned to break hard problems into simple steps, and I still work that way.
>
> These days I build and launch my own products and do client work: a **live App Store app**, a **multi-vendor marketplace** with payments and escrow, and an **AI app in production** for a care-sector client. I also build **machine-learning projects** in Python, and I'm currently learning **Go**.

- 🚀 **Now:** Building AI-powered web and mobile products, solo and for clients
- 🌱 **Learning:** Go (concurrency, REST APIs and tooling), while keeping my other skills current
- 🧠 **Also into:** Data science and ML engineering (XGBoost, SHAP, FastAPI, Streamlit)
- 💬 **Ask me about:** React Native and Expo, Next.js, Supabase/Postgres, payments, and shipping to the App Store and Google Play
- 🤝 **Open to:** Full-time, contract and freelance roles in **full-stack, mobile, AI products or data science**
